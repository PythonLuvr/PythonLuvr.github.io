LOS Uploader

A private publishing tool.

LOS Uploader is a small command-line tool its owner uses to upload finished videos to their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file, with its title, description and tags, to the owner's channel.
  • Reads the name of the signed-in channel so it can confirm it is uploading to the right place.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments or account data.
  • It does not collect data from viewers or from anyone other than the owner.
